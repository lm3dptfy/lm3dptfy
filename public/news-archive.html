How Claude's Text Watermark Works (Aug 14, 2026)
Claude will embed an invisible watermark in generated text to indicate AI involvement, complying with EU AI Act requirements. The watermark works by subtly altering the randomness source when choosing between equally valid word options, leaving a detectable pattern that doesn't affect output quality or readability. No performance impact, no additional costs, and outputs remain indistinguishable from unwatermarked text — the watermark only enables later verification of Claude's involvement through a detection API Anthropic plans to release.
