🖨 FDM Print Quality

All items are produced using FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling) technology. FDM prints are built layer by layer and may exhibit visible layer lines, minor surface texture, or slight dimensional variation. These are normal characteristics of FDM printing.

Our prints are not manufactured products and will not have the surface finish of injection-molded items. By submitting a request, you acknowledge and accept these characteristics.
